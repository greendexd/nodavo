# Code of Conduct

Nodavo contributors and participants must create a respectful, technically rigorous, and welcoming environment.

## Expected behavior

- Discuss ideas and evidence rather than attacking people.
- Assume good faith while remaining precise about security and correctness.
- Welcome different experience levels, languages, backgrounds, and accessibility needs.
- Give actionable review feedback and acknowledge uncertainty.
- Protect private information found in reports, logs, and test artifacts.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, discrimination, threats, sexualized conduct, deliberate humiliation, doxxing, spam, impersonation, security abuse, and knowingly misleading technical claims are not accepted.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit or remove content, close interactions, restrict participation, or ban participants when necessary to protect the community. Enforcement decisions should be proportionate, documented privately, and avoid unnecessary disclosure of personal information.

Until a dedicated conduct mailbox is published, use [GitHub's private reporting form](https://github.com/greendexd/nodavo/security/advisories/new) and begin the title with `[CONDUCT]`. Reports are visible only to repository maintainers. Do not post personal information or the report itself in a public issue. This interim route will be replaced before the first public beta.
